# UOWD Assistant (static demo)

Unofficial student prototype for CSIT205 at the University of Wollongong in Dubai. Not affiliated with or endorsed by UOWD.

Ask a question about UOWD. The answer is built in your browser from short excerpts of public pages on uowdubai.ac.ae and always links to the original page. Nothing is sent to a server, no personal data is collected, and there is no AI service or key in this version.

Always confirm fees, dates and entry requirements on the official website. Do not enter personal data.
